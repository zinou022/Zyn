## Hard rules (same as before)
- Real screens captured from the running app on the local demo backend (demo data only, e.g. project PSM 00013). No mock-ups, no invented UI.
- Human-sounding neural voice (the same Kokoro voice used for the Portal video, same speed). Never a robotic voice.
- 3840x2160, 60 fps. Report resolution, fps, duration, size. Say honestly that you have not watched it through unless you have.
- Do NOT deploy, do not touch production, do not touch the Incident data/history question.
- Keep the wording decisions already made by zeyn in the earlier videos (no "FRD" on screen, "Zyn" instead of "System Owner" in the narration, etc.). Where the FRD says "system owner", follow those earlier decisions.
- Screens that do not yet exist in your earlier captures (Boarding, Equipment, OBS, Task Instruction, Incident, Audit and KPI) must be captured from the app, not drawn.

## BUTEC identity (the video must look like BUTEC, not like a generic app demo)
1. Open https://www.butec.com in your browser. Extract: the logo (SVG, or the one already used in the Portal footer), the exact brand colours (hex), the fonts, the photography/graphics style, section layout, tagline.
2. Build one design system from it and use it for every title card, lower third, transition, diagram, caption and end card:
   - brand colours as the palette (primary for backgrounds and titles, accent for highlights and callout boxes);
   - brand typeface (if it is a web font, load it; otherwise the closest free match, and say which);
   - logo top-left on every slide, safe margins, clean generous white space, restrained corporate motion (slow fades and slides, no bouncing);
   - the website's feel: confident, engineering/construction, wide hero-style title cards, subtle grid or line motif if the site uses one.
3. Recolour the workflow diagrams to the brand palette. Keep their meaning: user acts / reviewer acts / automatic / final / returned. Originals are in `video-brief/workflow-diagrams/` (the same figures as in the FRD, in order: 1 overview, 2 legend, 3 portal, 4 boarding, 5 statistics, 6 KPI, 7 OBS, 8 equipment, 9 task instruction, 10 incident; file names image1..image10 follow the order they appear in the document, check each one). Redraw them natively in the video (HTML/SVG), animated step by step in sync with the narration, not as static pictures.
4. If butec.com cannot be reached, fall back to the Portal's own BUTEC theme (it already carries the brand) and tell zeyn.

## Structure (about 6 to 7 minutes). Narration is a draft: keep it simple and human, adjust only for timing.

| # | Scene | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | BUTEC logo, "HSE Integrated Platform", tagline from the site | "This is the BUTEC HSE Integrated Platform: one place for the HSE work of every project." |
| 2 | Where the idea comes from | Quick montage: Excel sheets, PDF checklists, Word reports (simple graphics, no real company data) turning into one Portal screen | "HSE information used to live in many places: monthly statistics in Excel, KPI and audit checklists in PDF, investigation reports in Word, observations and worker documents in files and emails. Every project reported in its own way. Follow-up depended on people chasing each other, and information was lost when a person moved or a project ended. The platform replaces this with one controlled system: one login, one set of data, one way of working on every project." |
| 3 | Three goals | Three brand-coloured cards: Centralisation, Automation, Retention | "Everything we ask of the platform comes from three goals. Centralisation: all processes, users, projects and results in one place. Automation: the system assigns, reminds, calculates and reports, so people do the work and the system does the chasing. Retention: nothing about a project is ever lost. Records are voided or archived, never erased, every change is traced, and everything is backed up." |
| 4 | The Portal | Real screens: login page, then Portal home with the application cards, project tiles, notifications, search. Then the Portal workflow animated (project created, users and roles, one sign-in, only my projects and apps) | "The Portal is the single front door. You sign in once and see only the projects and applications that match your role. It holds the project register, the user directory, announcements and notifications, and the backups. The applications take their users and projects from the Portal, so data is entered once." |
| 5 | Boarding System | Real screens (workers, documents with expiry, medical fitness form, training attendance, pass and ID card, gate log, reports) + animated Boarding workflow | "Boarding controls who may enter and work on a project. Each worker has one record. Documents are registered with expiry dates and reminders are sent before they lapse. Medical fitness is recorded, and only medical roles see the details. Training is recorded, then the access pass and ID card are issued, and every entry and exit is logged. Transfers and offboarding keep the full history." |
| 6 | HSE Statistics | Real screens already captured (dashboard, reports list, assignment, report form, targets met or not, line comments, returned lines in red, reply) + workflow | "HSE Statistics replaces the monthly spreadsheets. Each month a report is created for every project and assigned automatically to the project HSE manager, with a seven day deadline. The system calculates the rates and checks the targets. The reviewer comments line by line, accepts or returns the report, and the assignee replies on the same comment. An accepted report can be reopened with a reason, and the earlier version is kept." |
| 7 | Audit and KPI | Real screens (HSE, HR and ADNOC checklists, score limited to the weight, missing lines in light red, total score, dashboard) + workflow | "Audit and KPI replaces the PDF checklists for the monthly KPI assessment. Each checklist keeps the original table, weights and scoring. A score cannot exceed its weight, a sheet cannot be submitted while lines are missing, and the total is calculated automatically. Review, return and reopen work exactly as in Statistics." |
| 8 | OBS Tracker | Real screens (observation form, public QR page, assignment, discussion, close-out, validation, dashboard) + workflow | "The OBS Tracker captures safety observations from site and follows each one to a validated close-out. Anyone can raise one, even through a public QR page. The priority sets the deadline: critical in 24 hours, high 72, medium 120, low 168. Late items are flagged and escalated automatically, and closed means evidence was provided and validated." |
| 9 | Equipment Management | Real screens (asset register, required documents, verification, mobilize, inspections) + workflow | "Equipment Management makes sure every machine and vehicle has valid documents and inspections. Each equipment type defines its required documents. The coordinator verifies or rejects them, an asset is mobilized only after verification, and expiring documents are flagged automatically." |
| 10 | Task Instruction | Real screens (catalogue, crew from roster, HSE review, public link or QR page) + workflow | "Task Instruction gives the crew a clear, reviewed briefing before work starts. A supervisor prepares it from a controlled catalogue, selects the crew from the roster, and the HSE team reviews it. The crew opens it by link or QR code, and when the task ends it is closed and kept as a record." |
| 11 | Incident Investigation | Real screens (event, severity, deadlines, investigation, line review, report) + workflow | "Incident Investigation manages an event from the first report to closed corrective actions. Severity sets the level and the deadline, counted from the event date. Facts, witness statements, root cause and actions are in one case file. The reviewer comments line by line, an approved report becomes a fixed copy, and the Word report is generated automatically. Events are never deleted, and the history cannot be altered. Incident figures feed HSE Statistics." |
| 12 | Documents produced and saved | Animated folder tree: Corporate (E&C) branch and Project branch, L1 then L2 folders, from FRD section 6. Real screen if the app shows documents, otherwise the animated tree only (a diagram of the FRD, which is allowed) | "Every document the platform produces or receives is saved by the system itself, in a fixed folder structure. Nothing is filed by hand. Each document carries the project, the date, the author and a version number, and nothing is ever deleted: a correction creates a new version. There is one Corporate branch for the whole company and one Project branch for each project, and access always follows the role and the project." |
| 13 | Closing | Logo, tagline, three goals as a line: Centralisation, Automation, Retention | "One platform. One set of data. Nothing lost." |

Check every scene against the FRD text before finalising; if the FRD wording differs from the draft narration above, the FRD wins.
